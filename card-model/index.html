Should a crypto wallet issue a card?
eMoney is a licensed Georgian e-money institution; Mycoins is its cryptocurrency exchange. In 2025 they wanted a Mastercard debit card with two paid tiers and Bitcoin cashback. The question was not "can we" — it was what the P&L looks like, when it pays back, and which tier carries it.
